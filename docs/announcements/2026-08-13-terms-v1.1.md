# Terms & Privacy v1.1 — effective 2026-08-13

terakota's License & Terms (EULA), Privacy Notice, and Security Policy move to
**version 1.1, effective 2026-08-13**. The live copies are at
[terakota.io/terms](https://terakota.io/terms),
[terakota.io/privacy](https://terakota.io/privacy), and
[terakota.io/security](https://terakota.io/security); the same texts are in this
repository under [docs/legal/](../legal/) and [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

## Why

The upcoming **v1.4.0** release adds support for connecting **production
QuickBooks Online** companies. Intuit does not permit the loopback OAuth flow in
production and requires a client secret no distributed binary can carry, so
production connections run through a hosted connect service we operate
(`oauth.terakota.io`), gated by a free terakota account
(`app.terakota.io`). The v1.0 terms truthfully described a product with no
service of ours in any flow; that stops being true in v1.4.0 for this one path,
and the terms now say so precisely.

## What changed

- The claims that were broader than the truth are narrowed to the truth:
  "no account needed, nothing sent to us" is now scoped to AppFolio and local
  use, with production QuickBooks named as the exception.
- New disclosure of exactly what the connect service holds for an account: the
  account itself and an eleven-field connection record (metadata only — never
  your QuickBooks data). Token material transits the service during
  authorization and renewal and is delivered sealed to your machine; no token
  the service could read is ever at rest on our side.
- The service dependency is stated plainly: while the connect service is down,
  fresh production QuickBooks reads stop once your access token expires;
  AppFolio and local work are unaffected.
- Security policy gains a safe harbor covering `oauth.terakota.io` and
  `app.terakota.io`, and an incident plan for the connect service.

## What did not change

- No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting — still zero.
- Your business data never reaches us. Reads run from your machine to the
  vendor directly, in every mode.
- AppFolio and local/sandbox use need no account and send nothing to us.
- The binary remains read-only by construction toward your business systems.

## Timing notes

- These terms are effective on publication. The current release (v1.3.1)
  predates production QuickBooks support; this repository's README will
  continue to describe QuickBooks as sandbox-only until v1.4.0 ships — that
  window is expected.
- v1.4.0 will show its first-run notice again (once) to reflect the new terms
  version, including for existing installs.
